# Whole-note withdrawals versus partial payouts and private change

Status: completed 2026-10-21; candidate rejected by the frozen arithmetic gate.
Research only. No native transactions, proofs or broadcasts are generated by
this comparison. Earlier native-usage and H17/H18 evidence stays unchanged.

**Result:** requested payouts with reusable private change improved the tested
amount score in the overlapping, independent-demand case. They did not deliver
the required improvement in the sparse case, and receipt-correlated partial
payouts made amount guesses more accurate. Retain the mixed result; do not
adopt this policy as a privacy fix or start its conditional native continuation.

## Results

These are successful guesses within 10% of the true amount, among original
honest-to-honest payments. Lower is better. Each entry is the strongest fixed
strategy's aggregate score, including the prior and earlier-prefix copies.
It is not a percentage of privacy or proof of unique disclosure.

The overlapping case meets every directional condition. Its 12.5-percentage-
point reduction survives final drainage. The whole-note winner is the residual
attack carried forward; the partial family's within-10% maximum equals its
proper prior. That equality does not establish zero information leakage.

The sparse case fails the amount-improvement condition for both observers at
both horizons: one fewer correct guess is only 2.70 points, and the pooled
final score does not improve at all. The partial arm's pooled `split_tail`
attack reaches 11/37 at final drainage, 5.41 points above prior 9/37, also
exceeding the allowed five-point advantage. The aggregate result identifies
the successful strategy; it does not yet show whether each hit follows the
actual target's change lineage or a coincidentally priced alternative.

The sparse pooled sender maximum rises 0→2/37 at next payout and 2→5/37 at
final drainage, failing the identity non-regression rule too. Arm-specific
priors already differ 0→2, so this is not evidence that withdrawals caused all
of that difference. Final `split_tail` adds successful sender guesses, but
causal attribution requires a separate exploratory diagnosis. The amount
conditions alone already reject the candidate.

not evidence that arbitrary independent partial cashouts reveal half-invoices.
Stress amounts did not decide continuation under the frozen contract, but must
remain visible. The sparse stress next-payout recipient maximum also regresses
0→2/36; that difference already exists in creation priors and must not be
described as demonstrated new withdrawal-induced identity leakage.

All 16 frozen decision rows completed; six fail. They are overlapping views
of four ledgers, not independent discoveries or a majority-vote decision.
The overall status is `candidate-rejected-by-arithmetic-screen`.

The 45-tKAS prior optimizes within-10% success, not exact matching; its zero

## Question and matched comparison

Does withdrawing only requested cash, then keeping reusable private change,
reduce the amount information exposed by whole-selected-note withdrawals?
The [frozen specification](WORKING-BALANCE-PRIVACY-SPEC.md) defines the complete
workload, attacks and criteria. [RFC-0016](../../docs/rfc/0016-working-balance-withdrawals.md)
and [ADR-0031](../../docs/adr/0031-arithmetic-working-balance-screen.md) authorize
an arithmetic rejection screen before any new wallet integration.

Four fresh economic histories each run under both policies: overlapping and
sparse activity crossed with independent and receipt-correlated cash needs.
Each has 32 roles, eight colluders, 64 private payments, 32 cash requests, two
urgent complete exits and final drainage. There are four paired ledgers, not
512 independent payment experiments.

Both arms have the same funds and economic obligations. Whole-note overshoot
stays owned and can meet subsequent cash requests. Partial payouts retain
private change for later spending. Previously requested cash is earmarked
outside the pool; this models accumulated native liquidity, not external
merchant spending. Re-entry uses only surplus beyond that earmark, publishes
its native-source ancestry and incurs modeled fees. No extra cover payments,
subsidy, compulsory delay or permission to exit is introduced.

Observers see public arithmetic events and the initial funding-owner directory
(C+D); the stronger profile also pools all eight colluders' permitted records.
All zero-payout SPENDs, including merges, enter the observable target set.
Only after predictions are sealed does scoring select original honest-to-honest
payments. Commitments and nullifiers are idealized opaque labels, not actual
cryptographic objects or native transaction bytes.

## Attacks and prospective decision

Thirteen fixed strategies cover the price prior, payout-plus-fee residuals,
known co-input subtraction, payment/change splits, one-to-three-payout sums,
partial tails, receipt correlation, colluder tags and re-entry ancestry. Pooled
observers retain unchanged C+D strategies; final observations retain unchanged
creation and next-payout strategies. Report the largest aggregate success
count among fixed strategies, never a truth-selected best guess per payment.
The split strategies retain eight ranked supports per amount; bounded search
can miss deductions.

At both next public payout and final drainage, each independent-request
cell/profile must improve amount-guessing success by at least five percentage
points and remain within five points of its matched prior. Sender, recipient
and joint family maxima must not regress by more than five points, including
the correlated stress cells. Stress amount outcomes are reported separately.
Any failed condition rejects this candidate under this screen. Invalid
accounting or missing observations would instead invalidate the experiment.

## Decision and next evidence question

Stop this candidate's conditional native continuation. Retain the existing
partial-payout capability and the hypothesis that persistent private spending
can help; one favorable cell does not justify a default privacy policy.
The same mechanism can help, fail to help, or expose recognizable invoice
behavior under different cash needs.

The next useful investigation is to classify the sparse pooled `split_tail`
hits against actual receipt/change lineage and separate them from common-price
coincidences. Treat that analysis as exploratory, preserve this failed gate,
and do not retune it into a pass. A later candidate needs a separate prospective
comparison and a credible user-behavior assumption. The price-prior/absolute-
target conflict also needs explicit requirements review, not a silent metric
change. No wallet policy, protocol, key derivation, invariant or milestone status
is changed by this arithmetic result.

## Interpretation limits

This is a small falsification screen. Four paired ledgers cannot establish
repeatability, population anonymity or security. Sparse and overlapping cells
use independent histories; their differences are not a pure timing ablation.
Arm-specific opaque IDs affect role-prior tie-breaks, so identity-count
differences can include guess variation rather than new causal disclosure.

The known price distribution's prior has expected within-10% accuracy about
21.10%, already above the unchanged 20% absolute target. A 0.4-tKAS operation
fee is 4% of a 10-tKAS payment before any merge or re-entry. Fees divided by
large idle starting balances cannot qualify the adopted per-payment lifecycle
target. No result here establishes real proof/wallet latency, native acceptance,
backup recovery, network anonymity or cryptographic soundness.
