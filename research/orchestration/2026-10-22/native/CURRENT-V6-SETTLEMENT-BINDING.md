## Binding chain and evidence labels

Authorization::bytes serializes all 377 bytes: tag/domain, anchor count/root, two NFs, two CMs, mode, payout, fee, destination script, deposit, funding outpoint/script, bootstrap amount and ciphertext-pair digest (v6/mod.rs:68-92). The circuit hashes these same bytes and exposes the two little-endian 128-bit SHA256 limbs (v6/circuit.rs:275-278). The covenant hashes the actual transaction payload's authorization bytes and supplies those limbs, the actual proof bytes and its embedded VK to the native verifier (v6/covenant.rs:597-619).

A proof intentionally does not fix the current reserve outpoint, reserve balance or current settlement paths: saved authorizations can settle after unrelated activity. The native UTXO context, unchanged covenant and fresh public witness must enforce those current facts. Reusing an old proof is not a binding failure when its note remains unspent and its historical root is still authenticated.

## Three material limitations / unresolved coverage items

1. **Native guard isolation is incomplete.** Current controls cover the binding chain, but many mutate authorization bytes and thereby invalidate the proof. In harness.rs:697-715, payout-output targets output 0; it does not separately establish the continuing payout output's amount/script check with unchanged proof and authorization. The inspected suite also lacks an explicit native successor-code/VK substitution control isolated from replay reconstruction. The source checks are present; this is a coverage limit, not a demonstrated acceptance flaw. E1 is historical and no current-head execution was done here.

2. **A valid proof can bind unusable recipient ciphertext.** Correct encryption and delivery are outside the proved relation. Existing native acceptance plus wallet rejection evidence is retained in E4, and current wallet.rs:584 states this boundary. This is not an unbound native transaction; it limits what “valid private payment” can mean before recipient validation. The wallet must continue to withhold credit for rejected delivery.

3. **Offline descriptor/head/UTXO authenticity remains a caller prerequisite.** replay.rs:3-7 and wallet.rs:67-68 explicitly exclude chain acceptance/freshness and parent ancestry validation. Correct hashes, a valid proof and synthetic/resolved UTXOs do not by themselves prove an output currently exists or is unspent on the accepted chain. E3 proves only its recorded historical replay/receipt claim. Trusted-setup honesty and cryptographic soundness likewise were not audited by this binding review.

No implementation change or new experiment is proposed by this report.
