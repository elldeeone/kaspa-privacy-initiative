Sticky AB retains A/B's ambiguity only because both remaining owners are
honest in that fixture and the service is assumed to preserve the full original
basket/history. It prunes exactly C's consumed NF and approved output pair,
with no fresh owner authorization or tuple rerandomization. Pruning itself
exposes the departed member's original NF/output pair by set difference; in
this conditional case that member is already the colluder.

Two registered owners do not certify two honest unknown owners. Sticky AC
after B leaves exposes A through C's own material. Several other failure rows
still have twelve *global* assignments because D/E remain ambiguous; A's NF
and output pair are unique. Those twelve worlds are not a privacy score or
probability, and cannot conceal the targeted failure.

**The observers differ.** Public-egress receives all released candidates,
including aborted/orphaned ABC and stable complete output tuples. Coordinator
also knows owner cohorts, upload identities and C's own material. Confirmed-only
receives just the final currently accepted private batch/current exits. For
ABC→AB it retains A's 2/6 candidates; for a final ADE after aborted ABC it has
3/15 because that rejected ABC is outside its view. These different observation
sets do not cancel the coordinator/public-egress failure. Counts from different
owner universes are not directly comparable privacy scores.

Eight policy controls refused additions/omissions, uncommitted publication,
conflicting CF/fee/nonce requests, new-owner admission, a restored pending NF
in a second session and pruning from an unaccepted Exit. All eight final
cohorts satisfied the **ideal** consent/current-state/accounting kernel. ADE
can be financially valid while violating the sticky privacy history rule.

The positive path assumes an honest threshold service with private computation,
authenticated accepted spent-NF state, durable global history and ordering of
log commitment before any egress. One centralized model object represents
that ideal functionality; no actual MPC or durable storage is implemented.
Its lists are not a crash-consistency or Byzantine consensus test. A restored
wallet alone cannot bypass a surviving global index; the failure row explicitly
removes or bypasses that index. General cross-service enforcement is unresolved.

Conflicting user requests expose a private-service liveness limit: the service
refuses to change a still-pending authorized payment/fee. Original direct Exit
remains a separate modeled route; the queue is no native debit or note lock.
The control does not repeat actual backup/exit software tests. Proof validity,
share opacity, consent, state authenticity and recovery remain explicit oracles.
The public fixture itself is not secret from readers. No native transaction,
real recovery, network observation, GPU work or financial action occurred.
Fees remain unmeasured; the frozen 21-KAS 2% all-in FAIL is unchanged.
