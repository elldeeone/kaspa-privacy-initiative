# Proposed R0 settlement experiment 01

**Decision requested for ADR59:** build a fresh, unfunded wrapper policy for exactly two scheduled compact private payments, with one established RISC Zero Groth16-compressed outer proof. The new policy explicitly authorizes virtual child transitions and checks their combined actual native body. It is not an upgrade of the old pool, asynchronous reusable intents, or adoption of vProgs. No outer proof should run until the host contract and native body oracle pass.

The ordinary public gate is now implemented: the pinned B05 verifier performs actual G16 checks; the relation harness checks the ordered public transitions, IDs, NF/CM updates, reserves and bodies. The unchanged old-policy aggregate route fails by construction and remains a negative. The new policy below is a different, opt-in experiment. Its semantic contract is [VIRTUAL-P0-CONTRACT-01.md](../settlement/VIRTUAL-P0-CONTRACT-01.md); this plan makes the prover/native boundary concrete.

## Concrete contract

1. **New authority:** an actual supplied starting UTXO `Q0` is governed by wrapper script `W`, carrying child state `S0` and immutable wrapper domain, child domain, child VK, child virtual-template identity and outer program/key pins. Native independently binds `Q0`, `S0`, input reserve `R0` and its own script template. Child domain/key choices are never caller-selected. Old funded UTXOs cannot migrate through this branch.
2. **Minimal children:** exactly two compact-role mode2 statements, both `payout=0` and zero payout SPK. Each has its ordinary private ownership/conservation proof. The first Auth previous point equals actual `Q0`; the second equals `id(V0):0`, where `V0` is the exact canonical child transaction described below. Auth1 prior state/reserve must equal Auth0 successor state/reserve. Native final payout list is exactly empty, not an omitted unchecked list.
3. **Explicit virtual projection:** `V_i` uses the immutable **child template C**, not actual wrapper W, with the child Auth, public NF/CM/cipher records, one child-template successor output and canonical v1 fields. Its real keyed-BLAKE3 ID is independently reconstructed. The outer predicate verifies both actual G16s plus all native-owned strict NF-absence/insertion, CM-placement/emptiness, ordered digest, range and conservation rules. It rejects cross-child duplicate NFs. A future batch starts at its actual wrapper outpoint `Q1`; no virtual ID is stored self-referentially in W.
4. **Exact fee equation:** quote the complete outer body and allocate positive integer child fees `F0,F1` with `F=F0+F1`. Clients then fix their output commitments/ciphers, exact virtual bodies and proofs for that prefix. Enforce `R2=R0-F`, each child reserve equation and each private conservation equation. Actual native transaction has exactly one pool input and exactly one W continuation output of `R2`; consequently its actual implicit L1 fee is exactly F. No sponsor input, hidden fee credit, fee clipping, refund or relayer-surplus output exists. A changed actual quote requires fresh owner Auths/proofs; old proofs are not edited.
5. **Native binding:** W reconstructs the journal from actual input point/value, its prior state, declared final state, exact transaction payload and actual output value/SPK. It verifies that output0 is its own immutable template with final state, requires canonical version1/native subnetwork/zero gas/zero locktime/one input/one output, and verifies the genuine outer G16 under the fixed RISC Zero key and expected image. The outer image is compiled before W pins it. Guest code must not embed a hash of W containing its own image: actual input/output SPKs are public body inputs, then native matches them to its current/successor templates.

Before owner proving, the host may solve a public mass/price fixpoint using a legal candidate and explicitly normalized fixed-width proof slots. That provisional allocation is not fee authority. Inner proof bytes appear in the final aggregate payload and therefore change its v1 ID; after all genuine inner proofs arrive, normalize the exact body and reconcile the actual quote before guest execution. The outer proof's signatureScript slot is excluded from the v1 ID. Recheck the final genuine outer body as well. A mismatch aborts/reprepares; neither a cached shape quote nor an old Auth can override conservation. Storage, all lengths and budget allowance are checked, not inferred from two valid predicates.

## Proposed public journal and body binding

Freeze before guest build, subject to review: 424 bytes, with no private note witness:

```text
KPX5VS01[8] || version:u16le || wrapper_domain[32] || child_domain[32]
|| child_count:u16le(2) || Q0[36] || S0[112] || R0:u64le
|| S2[112] || R2:u64le || F:u64le
|| SHA256(ordered_complete_child_records)[32]
|| SHA256(actual_financial_body_projection)[32]
```

Complete child records include exact Auth528/proof128 and all public NF/CM/cipher/update bytes under the implicit trusted compact role; no record sorting, omission or deduplication. The proposed native payload is exactly `KPX5VP01[8] || version:u16le(1) || count:u16le(2) || record0 || record1`, with each record the existing3920-byte mode2 P0 payload followed by its128-byte proof: total8108bytes. Child-template/header bytes are reconstructed from installed policy, not request-selected.

**The424-byte journal is reconstructed, never embedded in that payload.** The payload has no journal hash, body-projection hash, current transaction ID or outer proof. Consequently the journal may hash the body projection including this payload without a self-hash cycle. The body projection has one fixed encoding of transaction version, input/output counts, actual previous point, sequence, input amount/SPK, output amount/SPK and covenant absence, locktime, subnetwork, gas, payload length and exact payload. Native uses its introspection opcodes to reproduce that projection and hashes it. This is explicitly a **financial body projection**, not a claim that every serialized field is committed: signatureScript contains the outer proof, derived txID/storage mass are recomputed, and compute budget is validated/priced separately. No own-proof or own-image hash cycle is introduced.

Both guest and native enforce `F=R0-R2`; no guest-supplied fee or body hash is accepted without that independent native reconstruction. The precise canonical codec and every fixed-field native gate must pass differential mutation tests before a receipt is produced. Virtual v1 IDs use established Kaspa hashing code, never an invented BLAKE3 replacement.

## Fixture and fallback boundaries

The retained old-domain wide→compact public chain is useful as a **semantic unit control** for the general checker; it need not fit this compact/P0-only production shape. Its old child domain/template must remain explicitly pinned. A supplied synthetic W UTXO can test a separate body binding, but assigning it a retained old outpoint does not create its funding parent or prove migration/ancestry. A new financial profile needs fresh child domain, matching child parameters/VK, deliberate client consent to virtual scheduling, new wrapper identity and fresh actual Auths for its selected starting point. Use only disclosed unfunded synthetic notes; never aggregate actual owner witnesses.

W must retain a genuinely unilateral **private** direct-exit branch. It uses the actual current point, the owner's newly generated compact private proof and current public data, and native NF/state/output checks; no other child proof, operator, outer compression assets or future arrival may be required. Integrate the separately reviewed no-append private full-note exit relation only after its own gates pass. Until then ordinary compact fallback is explicitly bounded by CM capacity. REVEAL is a separately consented disclosure option, not the cheaper cost credited to a private lifecycle. Pending virtual receipts are not confirmed spendable claims before settlement; on abort, owners retain their original confirmed notes and may freshly prove their own fallback.

DA must provide the public records, delivery ciphertexts, state-update history and role parameter identities needed by every owner after operator disappearance. Inclusion/ordering, maximum waiting policy, stale batch rejection and reorg recovery remain explicit requirements. A hash commitment is not retrieval. This scheduled design requires clients to know the preceding public proof is valid before authorizing the next virtual point; it is not independent asynchronous admission.
