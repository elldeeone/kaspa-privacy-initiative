# Independent sample sizing and heldout gate

**No new holdout is selected.** Original qualification already fails the known
45-KAS amount prior (>20%) and sparse single-source cases; human identity is not
modeled. A larger sample cannot repair those analytic facts. The finite public
controls run first, and a genuine changed protocol/behavior must be specified
and implemented before any empirical improvement study.

## Sampling unit and inference

One independent unit is a freshly sampled legitimate demand/funding history,
not a view, action, note or paired delivery variant. Pair all compared behaviors
on exactly that unit's obligations, values, user deadlines, withdrawal demand
and actor facts. All public action partitions and metadata remain visible.
Change, padding, fee credit, self-churn and future arrivals are not useful
independent demand. Sparse/deadline-expired cases remain explicit failures or
fall back at their actual privacy/fee cost.

A future target must name the adversary output and its baseline: application
payer-role guess, funding ancestry, exact CM-to-cashout link or principal guess.
These are distinct metrics. Human identity and cryptographic owner-world
indistinguishability cannot be substituted with role labels or SAT counts.
Original absolute gates and prior failures remain visible even if a particular
fixed attack improves.

## Power must follow the target

For a fixed family M of simultaneous comparisons, alpha=.05 and independent
cluster fractions in [0,1], the distribution-free two-tail Hoeffding radius is

```
r = sqrt(log(2*M/alpha)/(2*N)).
```

Paired differences have range [-1,1], so their radius is twice as large. For
illustration, M=64 and a 5-percentage-point radius require at least1570 independent
units for fractions, or6279 for paired differences. These are planning examples,
not a selected family or permission to draw. Using 96 repeated views as N=96,
or using N=4 with a radius above1, cannot certify a20% target. A finite study
also cannot prove a literal population probability of zero forced links.

If a future candidate justifies an empirical study, enumerate the exact family
before data. Prefer an exact binomial/paired analysis for a single precommitted
honest target per independent history where its assumptions hold; compute its
sample size and power from a declared null/alternative, not observed outcomes.
A target chosen by hidden truth never becomes an attacker eligibility mask:
the observer must emit predictions for every public position first.

A public runtime pilot, not hidden outcomes, must establish that complete global
queries fit the agreed CPU/memory envelope. If the required N or full attacker
coverage is infeasible, report that limitation or use a deterministic
falsification claim. Do not reduce N/observer strength or select favorable
completed cells after looking at a holdout.

## Future freeze requirements

Root and an independent adversary reviewer must bind all of the following
before any fresh OS sampling seed is drawn:

1. Implemented candidate/version, invariant-preserving statement, current
   source pins, exact public partitions/modes/fees/delays and fallback.
2. Threat models, legitimate participant invoices/payer records/NFs and any
   coordinator/submission metadata; strongest declared profiles included.
3. Complete workload, independent sampling unit, selected N, comparisons,
   original gates, confidence/power calculation, unknown and stop rules.
4. Producer, public-only attacker, independent certificate/truth checker,
   scorer/aggregate source and resource limits, including full/partial and
   one/two-real policies and current no-append mode where applicable.
5. Fresh independent sampling commitments outside Git, separate cryptographic
   material, no seeds/keys/private maps in attacker inputs; all predictions
   durably sealed before any scorer gets hidden truth. Every original case
   remains; no retuning, regeneration or retry after outcomes.

A result may establish an actual scoped improvement against a fixed attack or
falsify a proposed design. It never becomes general privacy by ignoring the
known analytic failures, incomplete cells, missing ownership/crypto equivalence
or unimplemented protocol dependencies.
