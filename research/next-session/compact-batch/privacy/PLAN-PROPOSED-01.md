**Not selected or executed.** Root and an independent reviewer must approve this

## Question and claim boundary

Does a demanded32-recipient batch change what a public observer can infer about
positioned private notes, later native payouts and original funding, compared
with the same payments delivered through32 two-output private transfers?

A one-payer batch cannot hide its original funding lineage merely by shuffling
32 outputs. Include that as a fixed public negative control. The heldout study
uses two independent payers; a single batch's permutation ambiguity must never
be reported as sender anonymity.

This is a new **synthetic public arithmetic relaxation**, not a native/proof
execution, human-ownership model, hash-preimage construction, indistinguishability
proof or privacy qualification. The current matched circuit permits two real
inputs. The tested wallet workflow uses one real input, so every forced result
is conditional on the explicitly known honest **one-real-input wallet policy**.
No protocol-wide forcedness claim follows without a separate two-real relaxation.

Every private action must have at least one positive private output OR a
positive authorized native payout; all-dummy/no-payout actions are invalid.
The new observer is global and exact within that scope. The previous12-note
observer, all old72-case results, prior sampling source and existing21.09772557%
common-price failure remain unchanged. Their source may be studied, not modified
or silently rerun under new bounds.

## Two observation horizons and three side-information profiles

Each ledger has a prefix ending immediately after its last private payment and
a complete-drainage horizon. Prefixes contain only funding and participant
records already available by that point. A fresh isolated attacker process is
used for every horizon/profile; no future view or earlier guessed answer is
mounted. Every attacker target comes from the public prefix, never a hidden
"economic payment" mask.

Profiles:

- **C:** public modes, amounts/reserves/fees, ordered and timestamped positioned
  CM births, sparse alignment/counts, opaque NFs, current genuine sealed-box
  ciphertexts, public funding-owner directory, schema/price/wallet policies.
- **C+1:** C plus the legitimate derived receipt values and positioned original
  NFs of preselected payee role2, plus its authenticated business-invoice
  counterparty role. No other owner key or unrelated sender identity.
- **C+8:** C plus pooled legitimate records of payee roles2..9. These are static
  participant observations, including the same legitimate invoice-counterparty
  records, not an adaptive active attack or endpoint compromise.

Participant keys/seeds and raw private receipts are not attacker files. The
trusted generator derives the minimum allowed facts. At a given horizon a
participant's known NF can identify its own public spend, exactly as an owner
could do. This sampled business workload gives each invoiced payee an
authenticated payer/counterparty role. This is application-counterparty metadata, not proof of original funding
lineage: even a known payer might spend value received from another funder.
**No counterparty-to-ingress-lineage equality is added to the solver.** The attacker gets only its own preselected participants' invoice
records, never unrelated recipient labels or the hidden global owner/source map.
These own-payment facts are legitimate known information and excluded from an honest-to-
honest diagnostic only by the later scorer. The observer receives no exclusion
mask. Actual participation, payment/value/withdrawal shares and zero-denominator
strata are reported; missing strata never mean privacy success.

Cipher slot i remains paired with CM slot i. The fixed historical byte31-highbit
and all-zero-cipher checks run on every public continuing cipher; any valid
format-derived fact can narrow the arithmetic model only if its interpretation
is declared and source-justified before execution. Current continuing dummies
use random rho and genuine seals to throwaway keys, not known zero CMs or raw
uniform264-byte padding. Terminal zeros are unappended/public. Failure of these
simple checks does not prove all classifiers fail.

## Public end-state consequences for solver performance

Only when the observed horizon ends atR=0, exact nonnegative conservation and
one-real/single-spend imply every positive Note was consumed exactly once. Spell
that consequence out directly rather than asking the solver to rediscover a
large pigeonhole proof. LetI be positive ingresses,A all private actions andK
positive private outputs. With the declared full-native-zero-output policy,
K=A-I. IfK equals all P0 output slots, every such slot is positive. A known
ingress value incompatible with every strictly later nativeP+fee must be spent
in a P0. If these ingresses exhaust all P0 inputs, every other positive Note
must match a strictly later nativeP+fee. The same finite-value consequence holds
for Notes born at/after the last P0. These are entailed constraints, not sampled
truth, guessed owner/input labels or added price priors. They are disabled for
openR>0 prefixes and would need separate review for two-real/partial-output
models. Independent certificates always check the unsimplified full ledger. For any
full-native cashout, each selected-position implication explicitly equates that
Note value to publicP+fee. This is already entailed by exactly-one selection,
consumed equality and zero private outputs; it exposes propagation without
removing an arithmetic world.

## Exact symmetry optimization, never sampling
