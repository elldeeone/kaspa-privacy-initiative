**61 of 96 observer views completed; 35 remained incomplete.** All 96 observer views of the 16 modeled
ledgers passed the separate keyless compatibility checks, after all 96
predictions had been durably sealed. No study source, workload, attack,
threshold or resource cap changed after sampling; no case was retried.

The 16 paired ledgers share four independent workload clusters and 256 unique
payment obligations. This is a synthetic, one-real-input arithmetic study of
**VARIED_POSITIVE_FUTURE_WALLET**. Current-demo financial privacy, native
acceptance and cryptographically realizable alternative owner histories remain
unqualified.

## Observed conditional results

- In complete public-C staggered wide views, funding ancestry was forced for
  all 64 early withdrawals across four clusters, and 64 of 256 complete-drainage
  withdrawals. This identifies an original modeled funding ingress, not a human.
- In each complete wide C+1 cell, the fixed same-birth counterparty heuristic
  guessed the payer role correctly for 248 of 248 other modeled payment outputs.
  That is a scored application-role guess under the single-issuer workload,
  not a logically forced cryptographic owner or human-identity result.
- No unknown-participant economic Note had a forced exact CM-to-cashout edge
  in the completed cells. Arithmetic SAT alternatives do not establish
  cryptographic ownership alternatives, equal probabilities or anonymity.
- The fixed 45 KAS guess was within 10% for 55 of 256 unique payment principals.
  The unchanged analytic prior remains 21103/100025 = 21.0977%, above the
  original 20% gate. The separate current uniform 32-note control still makes every
  amount forced from the known ingress, fee and equal-value wallet policy.
