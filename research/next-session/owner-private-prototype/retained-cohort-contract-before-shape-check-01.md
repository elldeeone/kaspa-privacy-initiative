## Primary statistic, fixed before the cohort

For world i, record the presealed number of admissible **unlabelled honest
partitions** K_i. Record success S_i=1 exactly when both eligible honest groups
are recovered, irrespective of a swap of honest creator labels; otherwise 0.
The existing one-payment/one-change source should leave two eligible honest
groups and K_i in {2,3}. Unexpected counts/shape are retained as INCOMPLETE and
investigated; they are not silently converted to misses or removed.

The comparison baseline assumes an ideal uniform hidden grouping among those
K_i candidates: p_i=1/K_i. It is an explicit model, not a proven property of
the real protocol or a lower bound on an adversary's knowledge. Compute the
exact Poisson-binomial upper tail for sum(S_i), using rational arithmetic and
the observed **presealed** K_i values. Flag evidence of excess success at
**p <= 0.01**. Do not retune the strategy, threshold, cohort size, exclusions or
denominator after observing results. Coordinator label/group counts and
colluder exact-label counts are descriptive secondary results.

A unique constraint-derived unknown grouping, concrete direct grouping
certificate, honest premix disclosure, financial acceptance failure or map
reveal fails the candidate independently of the statistical test. A correct
tie-break among several viable candidates is retained as a success in the
statistic, rather than described as a deterministic leak or discarded as luck.
