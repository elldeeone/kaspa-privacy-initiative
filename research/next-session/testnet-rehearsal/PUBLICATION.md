# Confirmed TN10 publication boundary

This packet contains the finite testnet adapter/constructor/lifecycle/recovery
source, retained local checks and failures, official source pins and the completed
six-body TN10 rehearsal's public evidence. Its 102-file accepted-01 extraction
was independently audited before packaging. Original full RPC responses stay
local; the extraction map hashes them and names the selected public excerpts.
Only unrelated FullV2 history `pages` are omitted. Derived phase archives bind
the excerpt file hashes explicitly.

Fresh trial seeds, private profiles/backups, private input witnesses/vectors/rho,
proving keys/caches and executable ELF files are excluded. Three binary protocol
artifacts in accepted-01 are public: two 328-byte VK copies and one 128-byte proof.
The two deliberately public historical known-answer JSON fixtures are compiler
inputs, marked DO_NOT_FUND; they are not the fresh trial's private identities.
Root's PRIVATE-EXCLUSION-CHECK.json records an additional private-value comparison
without publishing those values. Independent public auditors did not read seeds.

The executed adapter, chain driver, receiver and recovery checker remain source-
identical to their release pins. The reusable exporter was tightened afterwards
to exact driver filenames and 16 known generated-source paths; that source change
was tested without rerunning the trial or re-extracting accepted-01. Publication
filename checks are not a generic detector for secret content in arbitrary files.

Earlier local-only recovery reports and their closed manifests retain their
original bytes and scope. The additive network-closure-01 records the later
accepted-node observations. Source/runtime manifests can name local binary/key
paths; those are artifact identities, not promises that excluded artifacts are
in Git. Never rebroadcast the used trial. A new trial needs fresh private identity
and its corresponding domain/setup.

The observed result is six accepted native bodies, both native test-KAS payouts
and zero reserve/positive liabilities through a trusted validating-node/archive
view. This small two-1-KAS-note liveness pilot does not qualify fees, financial
privacy, independent network recovery, durable data, setup integrity, an audit,
wallet resources or mainnet/production safety. Confirmation depth is an observed
blue-score policy, not immutable finality.
