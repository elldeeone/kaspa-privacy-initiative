# Cached verification: frozen initial experiment

Starting source bd0bb946; selected before new receipts or wallets.

1. Prove complete original wallet-packet validity once. Compare unconditional
   Succinct cache plus fresh composed settlements against full verification.
   Begin with retained public same-packet contexts, explicitly supplied-state
   diagnostics, not accepted ancestors or upgrades of old banks. Require exact
   economic equivalence, complete receipts and native Full under the new image.
2. On feasibility, use a fresh bank and real wallet processes for the full flow:
   deposits, private payments, accepted updates/retries, protected output
   registration, optional batching, complete cashouts, zero principal/liability
   and restored-key spending. Keep service observations separate from controller
   private commands. Measure fees, work and waits for the same complete workload.
3. On functional success, create a fresh identity in one command and use free
   test KAS for complete terminal broadcast/confirmation rehearsal. No old
   identity rebroadcast, real funds, mainnet, admin exit or paused MPC work.

The 128-byte static journal binds a fixed purpose/version/domain hash, pool,
wallet-verifier ID and SHA256 of the entire canonical Packet (mode, complete
operations/proofs/deliveries and complete intent). Settlement pins the cache
image. Current bank/state/effects, anchors and economic nullifiers remain fresh.
No boolean 'verified' flag or skipped economic check is settlement authority.

Require wrong/missing image/receipt/journal, malformed proof, changed packet,
pool/domain/VK/intent/recipient/fee, spent NF, unaccepted anchor, stale native
effects, replay and cross-context refusals at intended layers. Strict final
verification requires successful complete claims and empty assumptions, with
development/Fake receipts forbidden. Preserve failed attempts, do not weaken checks.

Charge cache creation, wallet work, composition/resolution/compression, discarded
work and native fees. Report cold/warm latency and break-even separately. Keep
the 2% complete-cost target; hypothetical valuation is not an invoice. A source
review or partial proof is not testnet readiness or a speedup measurement.

Freeze observers: native/protocol data; actual service registration/approval
metadata; participant/contact facts; labelled timing and later owner disclosure.
Controller-private instructions and note labels are not automatic service facts.
Count payout ownership separately from payment amounts. Same-host processes
are not independent people. Faster proof generation establishes no new privacy.
