## Other preserved material

The original 149-file private preservation inventory also contains source/configuration drafts, native/receipt binaries, parameters, machine-readable evidence and diagnostics. Importing that inventory wholesale would add raw evidence and executable artifacts rather than a coherent knowledge guide. Their category-level constraints are captured above and in the [coverage notes](coverage.md); the original bytes remain privately preserved.

Three retained patches cover five originally modified tracked paths: self-funded architecture/threat-model notes, takeover dependency configuration and the rejected MPC vendor patch. Historical invariant alternatives are recorded in the [architecture guide](architecture.md). The vendor patch remains rejected, uncommitted and outside this import; dependency drafts are not proof of successful runtime integration.

Existing source-only proposals, one-shot diagnostics, supplied-UTXO checks, successful software verification and accepted chain history retain distinct labels. These unfinished notes must accompany any future reuse of their components.
