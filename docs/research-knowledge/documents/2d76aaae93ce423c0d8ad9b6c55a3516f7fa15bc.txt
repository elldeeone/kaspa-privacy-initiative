# Public development fixtures for two-v6 host binding

The fresh test-only setup and both genuine wallet proofs completed successfully.
Each unchanged v6 circuit has 4,372,824 constraints. Both proofs were accepted
by Arkworks 0.6.0 and each encrypted output passed its recipient's decryption,
commitment and plaintext checks. No proving key or wallet file was serialized.

The fixtures use a new domain and a new single-party, untrusted development VK.
Their public wallet seeds are fixed do-not-fund labels in the producer source.
The common count-3 membership context is synthetic and has no accepted native
history. These fixtures do not qualify the original rehearsal instance, native
backing/current spentness, zkVM execution, a receipt, settlement or privacy.

- Binary SHA-256: `2a539045b073179cb24816bebb530f3215013882e80a68aeb675ec3f38faf390`.
- Fresh domain: `d47cb91ef9ac74dda71c7db1519381526c3dfac5f525b904f744b97a7df713bd`.
- VK SHA-256: `2a9acd1639fb7b454384c65fb317a139695f5a2cbb3ebd941dd13b5f6045a2f4`.

Public bytes are under `public/`. `manifest.json` describes the two 875-byte
bundles and 328-byte VK. The separate `derive_public_contract.py` independently
checks their exact byte profile, common anchor/count, four distinct active
nullifiers, ordered ciphertext digests and paired-record permutations. It emits
`expected-public.bin` (1,088 bytes), `expected-journal.bin` (42 bytes) and
`expected-input.bin` (2,846 bytes). This script does byte checks only; it performs
no cryptographic verification. Exact identities are in `independent-contract.json`.

`5d667b709f35191c6fc98e6aa0b79bd934e39a40ef4edfea582fcef3b06d4ac7`.

The one setup/two-proof process ran 204.282 seconds, using 1,816.213 CPU seconds
and 7,200,669,696 bytes peak cgroup memory. Its bounds were 12 CPUs, 14 GiB,
zero swap and 30 minutes. Setup took 22.554 seconds; the proofs took 16.373 and
16.120 seconds. Both pre-setup constraint checks account for most remaining time.
The unit is stopped, MainPID/ControlPID are zero, and its cgroup is absent.
`launch.json`, `resource-samples.jsonl`, `terminal-result.json` and `cleanup.txt`
retain supervision and identity evidence.

The earlier authorized source audit found no qualifying pair in the fixed
published rehearsal: its only complete-profile SPEND bundles are rows 6 and 8,
whose count/anchor differ. The new development fixtures preserve that limitation.
