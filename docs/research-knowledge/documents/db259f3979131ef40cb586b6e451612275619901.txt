# One bounded 32-role policy comparison

This is a prospective **synthetic arithmetic constraint model**, not accepted
native execution, proof validity, independent human ownership or privacy
qualification. The historical accepted-pilot result and padding canary remain
separate. Freeze all sources, inputs and this method before generation/scoring.

## Workload and compared paths

Draw eight independent ledger clusters from fresh, separately preserved request
and opaque seeds: four `overlap-independent`, four `sparse-independent`. Use
the unchanged `working_balance_model.plan` sampler only: 32 roles, eight active
roles, 64 economic payment obligations, 32 independent native cash requests,
urgent whole-owner exits at requests7/23, and a final drainage order. Do not
reuse that rejected policy's payout clipping, overshoot, re-entry executor or
working-balance rules. No favorable request, empty stratum or ledger resampling.
Before drawing those private seeds, run the fixed public development controls:
the published eight-metric cross-check, 60M fee boundaries, a shape-only opening
and metadata rejection canary, and one explicitly public 32-role development
fixture with eight independent-validator corruption controls. Its known seed
and results are separate from held-out ledger samples; no attack is fitted.

Each cluster uses exactly the same requests, identities, net starting capital,
payment amounts and intended timing in all three policies and at100/200/500
sompi per normalized mining mass. No mandatory delay or retained balance is
introduced. The three policies are:

1. Whole-note REVEAL: fulfill the exact requested net payout only when an owned
   note actually pays it. Retain an unsupported partial request as failed.
2. P0 rerandomize then REVEAL: fund a payout note `V=P+f_reveal>=60M`, the P0
   fee and zero-or-60M private change; reveal the newly created payout note.
3. Private EXIT: fund public `P` and the exact fee, retaining legal private
   change. Continuing exits append two CMs even with no positive change;
   terminal exits append none and insert two NFs.

Urgent requests drain the owner's entire current balance immediately, including
when its separate exact cash request is unsupported. A later original payment
may require transparent re-entry: consolidate existing owned native payout
UTXOs, preserve completed exact cash requests, charge preparation/admission fees
and admit the remaining proceeds. No fresh principal or payer is introduced.
Record every re-entry, tail, unsupported request and explicit revealing fallback.
Private exit or rerandomization failing a minimum-claim fee condition may use the
declared owner-consented REVEAL escape; that disclosure remains in every later
privacy horizon. Every unspent positive note remains a liability.

All three main policies use the corrected genuine sealed dummy format. The old
broken raw-random padding is retained as its separately measured baseline fact;
no extra population arm is selected. Notes, real positioned NFs, receipts,
encryption and native public keys reuse the existing V2 receiver/crypto bridge.
No Groth16 setup/proof or accepted native transaction/state is produced. Dummy
NFs and transaction identifiers are opaque model labels. Alternatives in this
arithmetic model are not alternate witnesses for fixed cryptographic hashes.

## Public inputs, disclosure and blindness

C receives every modeled operation, public value/script/fee/reserve, accepted
ordering and modeled minutes, appended CM count, public NFs and ciphertexts,
full revealing openings, ordinary funding/re-entry ancestry, source policies and
all32 role candidates. A declared public funding-owner directory covers initial
and re-entry funding. This is explicit side information; fresh addresses alone
do not prove owner identity. Do not label a fresh V2 opening with a role solely
because that role once admitted a note. Invoice V2 has fresh O/incoming/K/PK;
its stable delivery key is not a public Note168 field.

C+P adds complete records of participant0. C+P+A adds the pooled complete records
of roles0..7 and their preselected tag amounts. Senders know selected inputs,
output openings and intended recipients; recipients know owned receipts and
positioned NFs, with no gifted sender identity. Active roles are eight of32 but
their realized payment/value/operation shares are reported. Their fixed tag and
scheduling powers come from the unchanged sampler; there is no adaptive online
attacker. I/network and E/endpoint-compromise claims are outside this model.

Generator/controller files may hold hidden roles and fresh model material.
The observer receives only its exact C/P view in an isolated filesystem/network.
No seeds, truth, other policy/rate/ledger, secret eligibility mask or economic
purpose labels are mounted. Every public P0, including self-rerandomizations and
drainage operations, is an observer target. Only the later scorer identifies
economic payments. No actor/file label supplies an answer.

Payment-horizon observations use a streaming protocol: send a prefix ending
on each public P0, read/seal its response before sending any later prefix.
The observer gets no future events **or future funding records**. Cashout and
drainage horizons each use a fresh observer process. All predictions across all
clusters/policies/rates/profiles/horizons must be complete and sealed before a
separate scorer receives hidden truth. Publish label truth only after this
barrier; never publish seeds, receiver keys or plaintext generator state.

## Fixed attacks, scores and limits

Four source-frozen variants run without fitting:45-KAS public price prior;
exact opening/CM joins and permitted records; public conservation/residual
matching with deterministic temporal ties; later payout/fee inverse/timing
guesses. Public active tags and P-owned NFs are available. Arithmetic matches
are guesses, never forced facts or equally likely alternatives. Public opening
equality may establish a note value; economic payment semantics and legitimate
already-known exclusions are applied only in the scorer.

Retain all original RFC0012 gates:10% identity,20% amount,5-point advantage,
selective precision and zero forced-link requirements. Primary diagnostics are
honest-to-honest economic payments; sender, recipient, relationship, exact
amount and `10*abs(guess-actual)<=actual` are separate outputs, with selective
relationship precision at5/10/25% coverage. Report every stratum/count and
legitimate exclusion. Never score private note positions as human identities.
The exact original common-price prior21103/100025=21.09772557% already fails
the unchanged20% cap. No new workload, eligibility or target tuning can turn
this into a privacy pass. Historical private balances/funding sets need further
attack coverage; this fixed family does not close those claims.

Use independent ledger clusters for uncertainty. Report per-cluster counts and
one-sided95% Hoeffding bounds with a predeclared Bonferroni family of10,000
rate/advantage/selective comparisons. Eight clusters give wide bounds; absence
of a certified breach is incomplete, never success. All family members are
descriptive; none may be selected as a newly fitted winner. Missing generation,
control, prediction, validation or solver work is incomplete, never anonymity.

Every operation and all per-user fees are counted, including admission, native
preparation, urgent/full drainage, re-entry and fallback. Count each original
economic payment once; rerandomization, recycled native proceeds, capital and
self-transfers never enlarge the denominator. Report per-owner burdens and
authored/received economic volumes separately, with zero-volume users retained.
Normalized mining mass uses the pinned exact integer KIP9 formula and actual
modeled amounts, source-checked against the published eight native-body metrics.
The200/500 cases recompute fixed points; old rate100 authorizations are not
reused. New model contexts remain source arithmetic, not actual native bodies.
The known1%/2% fee failures remain explicit; no latency/hardware pass is claimed.

a60M output; new private payout works only when `f<=50M`. At500 the direct
