Not implemented: a private2-input/4-output ceremony that supplies every owner the same final exact-effects/NF list while avoiding ownership disclosure, premature signing, distinct per-owner transcript views, retry/cohort intersection or coordinator mapping. The signature cannot simply cover an arbitrary hash supplied by the servers. A future protocol must specify how each owner reconstructs or verifiably agrees to all complete effects, identifies its specific input authority, rejects unauthorized output/fee/exit destination changes, and binds root/domain/lane/action/input index to one final transaction. If required portions remain secret from an owner, their reconstruction needs a separately qualified mechanism and a clear consent rule; this prototype provides neither.

The sender already knows the recipient/address information it used; co-servers under full collusion learn shared witness values; network and retry views can still link. No stronger privacy claim follows merely from keeping the verifier key private.
