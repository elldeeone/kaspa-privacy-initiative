## Remaining conditions

The empty-suffix induction is valid given a valid singleton and every intended
complete successor relation: ADMIT appends at n, subtree mode consumes aligned
space at or above n, and REVEAL/terminal carry CM state. Target authentication
alone does not prove an arbitrary skipped gap empty. The conditional
`m+2*u<=2*n+1` argument is sound when u counts positive unspent positioned notes
and pending proofs enforce real/dummy consumption and exact output arity.
It gives NF headroom, not continuing private CM-exit capacity.

Neither matched relation exists. The 32 slots do not establish 32 independent
useful payments; that demand/receipt requirement remains explicit. Sparse
physical-position recovery, matched compact/wide keys and genuine proof tests,
all actual lifecycle fees, full-capacity private exits and privacy remain open.
The reviewed circuit-obligation memo
`d267923295d28467315e9d2809bdb4fc0e207bb1107a3367f16416e3e26efd25`
correctly preserves these conditions. Its size/RAM/PK estimates are unmeasured
planning estimates with FFT and allocation cliffs, not setup authority.

No reviewer native VM, prover, setup, signature, RPC or funding ran. The reviewer
used read-only artifact checks and pure byte/abstract-cost/integer analysis.
