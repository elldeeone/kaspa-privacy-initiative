# Next 32-role comparison: prospective method, not yet executed

This is a draft, **not a completed privacy evaluation or a freeze**. A new
PRIVATE_EXIT envelope must first have an exact public/private field contract,
source-pinned wallet policy and complete native fee quote. Otherwise a model
could manufacture privacy by simply deleting observer-visible facts. No such
model result may qualify the actual implementation.

## Fixed requirements

Retain RFC-0012's32 roles/eight adversarial roles and all original identity,
amount, advantage, selective-precision, forced-disclosure and resource targets.
Use independently seeded ledger/session clusters and a prospective development/
held-out split. Treat simulated roles as model roles, not independent people.
Freeze attacker traffic share, deposits, chosen-value/tagging and scheduling
powers, rather than inferring them from eight role labels.

Reuse the published common-price distribution as a separately named unchanged
baseline. Its21.0977% prior already fails the20% gate; do not exclude those
cases, choose a more favorable distribution after results or call an ideal
privacy envelope a pass. Other workload families must be prospectively named
and reported separately, never substituted for the failed baseline.

## Compared implementations and horizons

1. Current Note168/private spend/REVEAL: expose the actual opening, original
   positioned NF credential and payout script at each revealing cashout.
2. Proposed proof EXIT: expose every actual statement/payload field, NF,
   private change CM/cipher, reserve change, native payout amount/script,
   fees, arity/padding and timing from the final contract. Hide only fields
   actually hidden by the selected proof and established cryptographic model.
3. Each ordinary historical target remains in scope through selected emergency
   REVEALs, individual cashouts and complete drainage. A failure is not removed
   by restricting the successful horizon or relabeling an ordinary target.

Use the same independently generated intended payment schedule in each paired
ledger where both implementations can actually execute it. Preserve all payment,
merge/change, retry, fee and final-tail outcomes. Invalid/stranded/unsupported
paths remain explicit failures or incompleteness, not additional anonymity.
Do not impose a new mandatory balance, self-transfer or delay policy to obtain
an apparent improvement. Count principal once and every attributable cost.

## Observer/truth split

The attacker runs in a sandbox with only its exact exported C view and declared
P/A additions. It receives stable role candidates and all public funding-owner
associations, distributions and wallet algorithms. The evaluator alone receives
private role assignments, notes/values and intended payment semantics. P gives
all declared counterparty records; pooled disclosure is separate from individual
participants. A controls the frozen roles and traffic/scheduling share. I needs
actual scanner/RPC/broadcast observations and is not claimed by chain simulation.

An opaque commitment/NF model is a computational abstraction, not a claim that
arbitrary arithmetic alternatives satisfy fixed cryptographic hashes. No
uniform posterior is assigned to feasible alternatives. Exact logical bounds,
forced public equalities and guessed labels/amounts have separate outputs.

## Attacks and evidence gates before scoring

Freeze a source-hashed attack family covering CM/key equality, deterministic
change position, input/exit arity, conservation and public amount/timing joins,
participant exclusion, distinctive active amounts and retained orphan disclosure.
Choose/lock attacks on the development split before held-out scoring. Exact
solver unknown/timeout and missing data are incomplete, never private. Include
canaries showing every attack recognizes its intended planted disclosure.

Report full-coverage sender and recipient accuracy separately, relationship
precision at5/10/25% eligible coverage, exact/narrow amount diagnostics and the
unchanged integer `10*abs(guess-amount)<=amount` success gate. Eligibility,
ranking/tie rules and legitimate already-known exclusions are frozen before
evaluation. Use one-sided95% upper bounds clustered by independent ledgers and
a declared multiple-comparison correction; insufficient sample support stays
incomplete. Do not average profiles/horizons into one privacy percentage.

## Inputs still needed before a new freeze

- Exact new PRIVATE_EXIT wire/authorization and source privacy boundary.
- Source-pinned ordinary spend/exit/fee/change wallet policy; supported failures.
- Actual complete native fees at100/200/500 and measured execution boundaries.
- A byte-conformant honest generator and separate observer export/scorer.
- Independently reviewed development/evaluation sample sizes and solver budget.

The current known-pilot canary is complete and remains unchanged. No32-role
simulation has been run in this workstream; no CPU load is created merely to
produce utilization while these load-bearing inputs are unsettled.
