# Native KAS privacy source checkpoint — 2026-10-27

This checkpoint preserves experimental source and design work. It is testnet
research, not a production release or a completed privacy system.

## Reported testnet results

The retained local rehearsal records report six payments, nine confirmed native
test KAS payouts, a zero terminal reserve, and three terminal wallet recovery
scans. Independent accounting covered nineteen transactions.

Total fees were **264,166,000 sompi (2.64166 test KAS)**, or **12.5793%** of the
fixed 21 KAS benchmark. The target is 2%; it remains unmet. Removing payment fees
alone would still leave 148,795,600 sompi of other costs.

These observations come from retained local experiment evidence. This commit
does not publish that evidence or establish fresh independent reproduction of
the rehearsal.

## Boundaries and unfinished work

- Payment grouping and the coordinator's knowledge of sender/output mapping
  remain privacy blockers. There is no new demonstrated native grouping-privacy
  improvement. Synthetic wallet-policy results do not establish general privacy.
- The three-party MASCOT scalar compatibility check succeeded over the required
  field. It does not qualify a complete private Groth16 prover, native batch, or
  fee saving. The new authorization prototype's first build failed; its repair
  and runtime controls remain unfinished.
- The final recovery-data publication audit, upload, and fresh downloaded-data
  verification remain incomplete. Local terminal scans do not establish durable
  independent availability of the complete recovery package.

## What this commit contains

The commit contains selected source, dependency declarations, design documents,
and bounded research summaries. Runtime JSON, transaction captures, raw logs,
archives, wallet material, and private experiment state stay outside this
commit. Existing research documents may refer to these retained local files;
those references do not mean the files have been published or audited for
publication. The raw RPC replay tool requires its separately retained local
capture. Archived circuit drivers also require separately prepared local tool
closures; this checkpoint does not vendor their executable dependencies.

The full experiment evidence, failed attempts, source selections, and operator
handoff are preserved locally. No research experiment was restarted for this
