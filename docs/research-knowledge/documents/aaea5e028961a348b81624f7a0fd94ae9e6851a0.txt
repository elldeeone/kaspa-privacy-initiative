## One shared state, direct exits and positions

History prefixes are 1,3,5 after founding/bootstrap/ADMIT. The pair's sorted
complete records append at positions 5..8; history must add both `(7,r2)` and
`(9,r4)`. Count 7 is an accepted CM prefix within one atomic transition, not a
separate reserve checkpoint. DRAIN creates no CM/history leaves. Both native
branches must update **one shared current NF root/count** and preserve the
entire same immutable policy in every positive reserve successor.

Current NF state, not historical CM membership, rejects spent claims. The
paired gate inserts all four real NFs in public raw-byte order, while indexed
predecessors compare little-endian numeric values. Later owner DRAINs insert
their own two real NFs against the then-current tree. A Bob authorization at
anchor 9 may remain unchanged across Alice's DRAIN, but Bob's public NF witness
must be rebuilt after Alice's transition. A stale witness or conflicting
reserve-outpoint spend must reject; a valid alternative owner order is not
itself a replay failure.

The direct branch must retain all ordinary bootstrap/ADMIT/SPEND/DRAIN rules,
not only paste a payout path into the paired diagnostic. After the pair,
Alice and Bob must construct genuine DRAINs using their own backup, public
records/state and fresh retained public proving parameters, with collector and
outer-prover access removed. Neither owner needs the other owner's keys,
hidden A/Q/grouping, a separate prover-side SDK receipt artifact or a privileged
signature. The paired transaction's public compressed outer proof remains in
the ledger for actual native replay. The generic owner CLI must not embed other
wallet seeds; mount only that owner's wallet, public ledger, public PK and code.
All test seeds are public outside the process boundary: this tests dependencies
and liveness, not secrecy. This remains a bounded local software experiment,
not independent-human or recovery readiness.

Public replay must follow every actual reserve outpoint, validate complete
transaction bodies and reconstruct the new S7 roots/amounts. The scanner must
parse both branch formats and process P's four complete CM/ciphertext records
in public order, preserving every occurrence by position. Equal records must
not be deduplicated: real NF derivation includes position. Existing old-v6
replay/scanner formats cannot silently accept the new descriptor/payload.
Public replay cannot call the private-input StateGate on P alone. It rebuilds
public NF/CM/history updates from P and authenticates their exact J/native
context with the public outer proof; it does not pretend to re-execute hidden
inner A/Q verification. Require the replay to work with A/Q unavailable.
[Position-based scanner](/home/luke/projects/kpi-intensive-2026-10-22/observer/poc/shared-pool/src/two_input/wallet.rs:300),
[old public replay boundary](/home/luke/projects/kpi-intensive-2026-10-22/observer/poc/shared-pool/src/two_input/replay.rs:1).

The stated capacity induction is valid as a conservative bound: each active
proof consumes at least one positive claim; SPEND adds at most two, ADMIT adds
at most two without NFs, and DRAIN adds none. Thus positive claims<=n-m/2 is
preserved from initialized genesis, also for two-proof pairs. If a positive
claim remains at n=65535, even m<=131068 leaves room for its two-NF DRAIN. This
requires authenticated reachable history; syntactic counts alone do not prove
it. The proposed small trace does not measure arbitrary capacity behavior.

## Identity and predecessor binding

Choose a fresh explicit inner domain, poolID and test setup/VK, separately
compiled fixed state guest and new image. Keep old compiled/default instances
unchanged. Inner authorizations remain 377-byte KPICU06 statements; domain
matching and the new VK exclude old-instance proofs. PoolID and image must be
fixed independently of prover input; J336 binds actual prior outpoint, prior/
next S7, P and native amounts. Shared NF state plus the actual reserve lineage
prevents replay through the other branch. A descriptor and authentic chain
view are explicit wallet trust inputs; the offline trace provides neither a
live accepted-chain guarantee nor global discovery of every same-script UTXO.

The proposed prefix stores poolID rather than inner domain. This is acceptable
only if every adapted ordinary domain comparison uses the separately fixed D;
blind old bytecode concatenation would compare A.domain to the wrong value.
Both branch successors must preserve the complete immutable suffix, including
both keys, image, selectors and direct exit. Removing the one-shot literal
designated-outpoint guard is a security-contract change; actual-outpoint J
binding and initialized tracked lineage replace it in the new experiment.

Avoid an identity cycle with this construction order:

1. Select public-test D/poolID and complete fresh inner setup/VK.
2. Compile the fixed state guest and independently freeze its image.
3. Construct/review the immutable native body with that image and keys.
4. Construct the supplied-UTXO founding/bootstrap/ADMIT transactions.
5. Derive pair J336 from the actual predecessor, then prove/compress it.

The guest cannot hardcode a not-yet-known founding/pair outpoint or the final
native-script hash. PoolID is chosen independently; native introspection binds
the actual predecessor. Fresh test proving parameters must survive until both
post-pair owner proofs are complete; the old discarded PK supplies nothing.
Use fresh OsRng for the setup, with toxic scalars/RNG state neither serialized
nor retained; only test wallet seeds are deterministic/public. Stream-save the
public PK and pin its hash/VK. This remains an unqualified single-party test
setup, with no secure-erasure or cryptographic-safety claim. Produce the first
four inner proofs for bootstrap/ADMIT/pair, then stop the producer/collector
and outer worker before isolated owners generate the two fresh DRAIN proofs.

## Minimal reviewable next stage and blockers

No setup/proof/execution is authorized by this review. The smallest next
reviewable stage, if separately authorized, is an additive pure public
state/codec/dispatcher/native-builder implementation with an independent
genesis-to-terminal oracle, **before** allocating fresh setup or guest proving.
Freeze the ADR/identity graph, exact two-branch ABI, source/lock hashes, new
script length/budget and required semantic controls. Reuse existing source
primitives; preserve every old format and one-shot artifact.

Blocking gates for a complete result remain:

- A real emitted shared policy with exact full-suffix continuation, stack and
  charge inventory; native whole-transaction and mass/fee qualification.
- One separately retained public-test setup and six genuine inner proofs;
  a separately pinned state-aware image/receipt under the fresh inner VK.
- Full public reconstruction/scanning through paired positions and both
  genuine isolated owner DRAINs after collector/outer-prover removal, including
  Bob's refreshed current NF witness and terminal payout.

The direct-SPEND control may reuse one new genuine pair bundle on a separate
offline fork; keep that fork distinct from the paired main lineage. Negative
controls must cover initialization, wrong identities/branch framing, altered
suffix/removal of exits, current-NF and outpoint replay, history/record
multiplicity, payout/amount/fee errors, capacity and native resource limits.

Successful bounded offline composition would not satisfy the adopted CPU/
memory/timing/fee/recovery/privacy requirements or authorize real funding.
Fixed 40M direct fees can fail higher research fee rates even if native script
validation succeeds. The separate higher-fee options note does not change that
target or this profile. No token, privileged rescue, old-pool upgrade, live
broadcast, archived private parameters or rejected experiment is involved.
