# Next experiment justified by the native-only result

The complete program and byte/mass screen fit the unchanged2% target only for
one genuinely demanded32-recipient batch followed by independently owned exits.
This is a candidate architecture, with no matched circuits or funded authority.
Two separate-time private-change payments still fail the target.

A separately authorized next profile would need, in order:

1. Exact compact2 and wide32 relations sharing the original positioned real NF
   derivation. Both bind Auth528 mode/digest/start/new count and current roots;
   the wide relation binds all32 private outputs in wire order, N-way derived
   CM distinctness and an overflow-safe aggregate bounded by reserve/MAX.
   Compact terminal gates must force every inactive Note168 field zero while
   preserving both native NF insertions and canonical Auth/cipher sentinels.
2. Adversarial circuit controls before setup: wrong mode/role, reordered/list-
   rebound outputs, duplicate CM, reused real NF across roles, wrong root/index,
   field/integer overflow, zero/dummy abuse, terminal nonzero outputs and payout
   mismatch. Genuine proof/native tests must then verify both intended roles and
   reject wrong-role reused proofs; zero-proof serialization results cannot do it.
3. Sparse receiver discovery, current-root replay and per-owner recovery for the
   new public wire, plus explicit public manifest/descriptor roles for both VKs.
   A recipient should need the compact proving parameters for its own exit and
   public verification data to inspect wide history, without another owner's
   private witness or the payer's wide PK merely to cash out.
4. Complete native entry→wide payment→independent exits with accepted proofs,
   integer storage/fee reconciliation and actual confirmed testnet payouts.
   Independently frozen privacy evaluation and the unresolved full-CM-capacity
   decision remain separate requirements.

No circuit implementation, setup, PK generation, owner input, RPC or funding is
authorized by this note or by the passing native-only screen. Existing circuits,
parameters, live trial identities and sealed evidence remain unchanged.
