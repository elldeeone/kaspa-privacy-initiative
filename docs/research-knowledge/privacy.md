# Privacy lessons

The [8 November Olaf comparison](../../research/olaf-privacy-comparison-2026-11-08/RESULT.md)
tests his published two-user TN10 deposit/private-transfer/sequential-exit
record. Known payout owners and one known contact force its single 4-KAS
payment amount exactly after 4/6-KAS principal withdrawals; the 0.5-KAS rent
refunds are separate. The public fixture also spells out the roles and amount.
Our current exit fields have the same arithmetic result on that *unexecuted*
matched workload. The actual 47-KAS branch result below is a different offline
history; 1/1 versus 4/7 does not rank the designs. Full public operation bodies
and independent-user chain-only ownership measurements are unavailable from
Olaf's publication. The optional service timing gain remains separate.

The [8 November note-granularity correction](../../research/note-granularity-privacy-2026-11-08/RESULT.md)
uses the actual wallet's one-input transfers and one-note EXIT rule, public
packet shapes and known-owner/contact auxiliaries. In the 47-KAS offline
history, **four of seven actual payment amounts are exact**, despite the
earlier balance-only screen's zero-exact necessary bound. Four structural
amount histories retain truth; they are not proof of cryptographic transcript
indistinguishability or real-world anonymity. A's truthful participant
disclosure collapses the fixture to one. A hypothetical two-input merge widens
fine-grained arithmetic possibilities but changes no exact fact or whole-KAS
history count in this workload; do not implement it on this evidence. Native
fees and outer work already total an illustrative 8.2123% before unpriced
costs, so the conditional new cost campaign does not start.

The [matched four-wallet screen](../../research/matched-payment-privacy-2026-11-08/RESULT.md)
reuses seven genuine private wallet payments and complete offline drainage of
40 KAS principal. An offline board made from the same real blind replies is
9,259 bytes and passes the public checker. Under supplied owners/contacts,
necessary balance constraints force zero of seven individual amounts exactly;
a separately assumed whole-KAS grid fixes one. This larger history retains
balance-level ambiguity, but the board does not cause it: ordinary replies give
identical amount constraints. Extra public proof fields, timing and price facts
may narrow the possibilities. The exact board was not redeemed or accepted on
chain, and no matched upstream code-level advantage follows. Keep the board's
separately measured timing benefit optional and stop cost escalation for a
complete payment-privacy claim on this mechanism.

The [8 November known-owner screen](../../research/known-owner-withdrawal-screen-2026-11-08/RESULT.md)
uses the already accepted bundle trial and explicitly supplied payout owners,
initial principal and contact edges. After full withdrawal, public values force
two of four actual private payment amounts exactly; the other two share a fixed
net difference. Its board-free ablation gives the same result. This is a
strong-observer **amount** control, not chain-only ownership discovery, a
relationship score, a matched upstream run or a population anonymity estimate.
The bundle remains a narrow optional service-timing fix; do not escalate it as
a complete payment-privacy architecture on this evidence.

The [atomic-bundle diagnostic](../../research/atomic-bundle-withdrawal-2026-11-07/RESULT.md)
gives all three wallets one simulated public set of valid blind replies. The
same malicious service's per-client release schedule then yields zero exact
links and six possible owner assignments in the local service-only view; a
fixed order guess gets one of three. This is narrower than global timing or
financial privacy. Independent accepted-chain reads, actual propagation,
Sybil/known-owner controls and public withdrawal amounts remain adverse.

The [7 November separated-role test](../../research/separated-withdrawal-2026-11-07/RESULT.md)
retains six structural mappings for an honest service, but its frozen order guess
matched all three once. A malicious issuer's different round IDs identify all
three keys despite genuine final approvals and unchanged kernel acceptance.
The observer used actual service messages, not owner-local truth. Enforce the
existing common-configuration requirement before payout-key disclosure; final
approval is too late to retract a leaked key. The repair and statistical timing
study remain open. The privacy gate failed and cost work was not started. No
independent-machine or native-chain result follows from these same-host controls.

The [7 November combined path](../../research/cached-approval-verification-2026-11-07/RESULT.md)
keeps blinding state and native payout secrets inside each wallet process, with
no labelled redemption result exported to its driver. Actual registration-service
messages retain six allocations for three fixed eligible roles; one participant's
own key leaves two, and all-but-one collusion leaves one. The same-host controller
knows labelled redemption order and identifies all three. Do not silently treat
that private harness information as isolated service knowledge, or omit it from
the broader observer control. Published auxiliary owner controls also collapse
the experiment. This does not hide withdrawal amounts, independently test global
traffic anonymity or qualify complete financial privacy. Cached verification adds
no anonymity by itself.

## Current corrections override older pass language

The later [joint-withdrawal feasibility audit](../../research/joint-withdrawal-feasibility-2026-11-05/RESULT.md)
traces five retained public EXIT requests to their payouts by script bytes.
Batching/shuffling those requests does not conceal their published assignment;
post-exit mixing cannot retract earlier facts. A replacement common-set protocol
is unimplemented, not ruled out by current L1. No favorable complete-lifecycle
comparison ran by assuming anonymous registration or deleting public fields.

The subsequent owner-authorized [finite replacement](../../research/common-set-withdrawal-2026-11-05/RESULT.md)
uses a different common-set relation and real blind registration messages.
Known input owners retain six payout-key ownership assignments under its
declared view, or two with one legitimate participant; mapped output owners,
all-but-one collusion and leaked origin/randomizer controls collapse to one.
These are actual output-owner assignments under stated cryptographic assumptions,
not hidden note labels or a proof of network anonymity. Current native VM joint
and solo paths work for the supplied finite instance; real shielded state,
Full validation, costs, recovery and broader privacy remain unqualified.

The later [actual-wallet integration](../../research/payment-set-withdrawal-2026-11-05/RESULT.md)
executes the original wallet's private payment, recipient re-spend, encrypted
discovery and atomic Ledger. The same narrow ownership counts survive its finite
native boundary. It does not authenticate native state ancestry, upgrade the
old outer guest, establish network anonymity or hide known-owner drainage facts.
Its direct-route fee floor fails the unchanged 2% target; cost conclusions must
remain separate from the ownership result and the shared batching architecture.

The [current lessons](../RESEARCH-LESSONS.md) carry three distinct analyzer
corrections: inserted known answers, hidden-label ambiguity counted as privacy,
and a live observer's wrong contact edge excluding execution truth. They also
record why private change and common-reserve funding did not supply a durable
known-owner/full-drainage fix. Read those corrections before any old score.

The current corrected finite live model retains two complete gross histories,
while disclosing some payment facts. Neither general uniqueness nor a financial
privacy pass follows. Confidential on-domain activity and a later conversion
into ordinary transparent KAS are separate observation boundaries.

removes the identified roster API itself and uses genuine eligibility/credentials
through our own actual onion service. Retain complete operator logs and separate

expected amounts remain attacks. The genuine unequal-wallet drainage control
reconstructs 23/9-KAS payments after later owner maps; that case is host-kernel

Freeze the observer's legitimate knowledge, workload/truth commitment, target,
amount grid, priors, metrics and stop rule before scoring. Score observable
roles/events and verify hidden-label renaming leaves the financial score
unchanged. Require the complete candidate history to retain execution truth.
Separate value/relationship disclosure from commitment-position ambiguity.

unqualified even if no transcript information leaks. This is not universal
